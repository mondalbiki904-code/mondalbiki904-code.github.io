HushColor Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026

In short

HushColor works without an account. Your artwork and progress stay on your device. The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob at a few natural breaks, never while you color. We never sell your personal data. Crash reports are anonymous, and you can turn them off.

Stored on your device

Coloring progress, finished artwork, your Gallery, points and settings are saved on this device only. We do not receive them. If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android may include this data in that backup; you control this in your phone settings. Reset all progress (Me › Your data) or uninstalling the app erases it.

Crash reports

If the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a report to Sentry, the error-monitoring service we use. A report contains the error and technical trace, app version, device model, Android or browser version, screen size, language, memory state, a random installation ID and a short list of recent in-app steps (for example, which screen was open). It contains no name, email, contacts, location, photos or artwork, and the service is set not to store IP addresses. Reports are sent encrypted, used only to fix bugs, and deleted within 90 days. Turn them off anytime in Me › Your data › Send crash reports.

Ads

HushColor is free thanks to ads served by Google AdMob. A full-screen ad may appear when you leave the summary after finishing an artwork, and you can choose to watch a short video for extra Smart Finds. Ads never appear while you color. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ads SDK collects information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and app details, and how you interact with ads. We only see combined ad reports, never your personal details. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before ads use your data, and you can change that choice in Me › Your data › Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings at any time. Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Sharing and saving art

When you tap Share or Save PNG, the image goes only to the app or folder you choose. We do not get a copy.

What we never do

No sign-in, no ads while you color, no precise location, no contacts, and no selling of personal data.

Children

HushColor is made for adults and teens and is not directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences, and the app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can turn crash reports off, change your ad privacy choices where offered, reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, reset your progress, or uninstall the app at any time. You are welcome to contact us with any privacy question or request.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and in the app with a new date.
